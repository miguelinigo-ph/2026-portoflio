Profile

I’m Miggy — a multimedia designer and creative director focused on building visual identities, digital experiences, and creative systems that feel intentional, expressive, and distinctly their own. I work across branding, art direction, graphic design, and digital design, bringing together concept, visual language, and execution to turn ideas into work that people can see, interact with, and remember. My practice is rooted in curiosity and experimentation, with an interest in culture, people, and the small details that give a project its personality. I like design that can be polished without feeling overly serious — work that has structure, but still leaves room for character, play, and unexpected ideas.
