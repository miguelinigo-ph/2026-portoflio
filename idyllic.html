Project Overview

The 2026 member profile was developed as a deliberate departure from the group's previous visual direction. While the 2025 profile leaned into dark leather, punk references, and an edgier attitude, this year's direction called for something brighter, more playful, and more reflective of the members themselves.

I developed an office-siren-inspired concept with a pop of color, using the contrast between polished office styling and expressive color as the foundation of the identity. Each of the twelve members was assigned a color based on their personality and overall image. Rather than requiring elaborate styling, members were given a simple direction — incorporate their assigned color as a key point of the outfit, whether through accessories or a statement piece.

The concept was also shaped by the project's tight production timeline. Planning began in early January, with the photoshoot taking place on January 11. The direction needed to feel distinct while remaining achievable within a short period, allowing the members enough time to prepare their looks without compromising the overall visual idea.

As the sole Creative Director, I developed the concept, logo, and visual identity, and oversaw the creative process from planning and production through the final digital outputs. The identity was designed not only for the member portraits, but as a flexible system that could extend across the campaign's different content requirements.

The Member Profiles

The twelve member profiles form the core of the identity. Each profile uses the member's assigned color as a visual anchor, allowing personality and styling to influence the imagery while maintaining a consistent overall system.

The layouts were designed to make the profiles feel connected without making them identical. The shared visual language provides structure, while the individual colors and portraits give each member their own presence.

Final Output

The completed identity brought together the member profiles, campaign graphics, and supporting social media materials into one cohesive visual system.

Visual Identity

The identity translates the office-siren concept into a visual system through color, typography, composition, and graphic elements. The logo was created specifically for the 2026 member profile identity, giving the campaign a recognizable mark that could be carried across its different applications.

The color system became particularly important to the project. Instead of treating color purely as decoration, each assigned color became a way of visually distinguishing the members while reinforcing their individual personalities.
