01

PROJECT OVERVIEW

The 2026 member profile was created as a departure from the group's previous direction. The 2025 profile leaned into dark leather, punk references, and a more aggressive visual language. For this year's profile, I wanted to take the identity somewhere brighter, more playful, and more personal.

The concept started with the idea of an office siren with a pop of color. The contrast between polished office styling and expressive color became the foundation of the visual direction. Each of the twelve members was given a color based on their personality and overall vibe, then carried through styling, accessories, and statement pieces.

The project came together within a tight production timeline. Planning began in early January, with the photoshoot taking place on January 11. The direction allowed the members to prepare their own looks while keeping the full profile visually consistent.

02

MEMBER PROFILES

The twelve member profiles became the core expression of the 2026 identity. Each member was assigned a color based on their personality and presence, translated through styling, accessories, and statement pieces.

Individuality Within a System
Each profile was designed to feel distinct while following the same visual framework. Color, photography, styling, and composition worked together to create a cohesive set.

Profile Layouts
The photography was paired with custom digital layouts, using consistent typography, spacing, and graphic elements while allowing each member’s identity to remain the focus.

03

VISUAL IDENTITY

The identity translates the office-siren concept into a visual system through color, typography, composition, and graphic elements.

01 / MARK
02 / COLOR

Color became a way of distinguishing each member while reinforcing their individual personality and presence.

03 / TYPE

Typography, spacing, and hierarchy were kept consistent so the twelve profiles read as one visual system.

04 / GRAPHIC LANGUAGE

Graphic elements were kept supportive and flexible, allowing photography and member identity to remain the focus.

04

SOCIAL MEDIA

The system was designed to move beyond individual profile cards and work as a repeatable social media language.

Consistent typography, spacing, color treatment, and image composition made the profile assets adaptable across posts while keeping the campaign recognizable as a single identity.
